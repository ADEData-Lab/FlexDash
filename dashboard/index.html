About Domestic Flexibility

Domestic flexibility includes smart EV chargers, heat pumps, home batteries, and smart appliances in residential properties. These assets can shift their electricity consumption in response to price signals or grid needs.

About I&C Flexibility

Industrial and Commercial flexibility includes cold storage facilities, water treatment works, manufacturing processes, commercial HVAC systems, and behind-the-meter batteries at business premises.

Methodology & Data Coverage

How this dashboard was created

Data Collection

Data was collected from energy suppliers, aggregators, and network operators using standardised templates. Each organisation submitted information about their flexible asset portfolios and service participation.

Data Governance

To protect commercial confidentiality, all data undergoes statistical disclosure control. Values are only published when 3 or more contributors exist for that metric. This prevents identification of individual organisations.

k = 3 Minimum contributors per cell

Illustrative Data

Where fewer than 3 contributors exist for a dimension, illustrative values are shown. These are based on sector benchmarks and are clearly marked with an orange badge.

Illustrative = Estimated value
Real = Actual aggregated data
